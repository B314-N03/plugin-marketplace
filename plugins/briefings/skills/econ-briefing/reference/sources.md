# Econ Briefing Source Strategy

## Primary Search Queries

Run these searches in parallel to cover the landscape. Always append a time qualifier like "2025" or "this week" or "March 2025" based on the current date.

### Core queries (always run)

1. `Federal Reserve OR ECB OR Bank of Japan interest rate decision`
2. `CPI inflation OR employment data OR GDP release site:reuters.com OR site:bloomberg.com OR site:cnbc.com`
3. `tariff OR trade policy OR sanctions economic impact`
4. `stock market OR S&P 500 OR Nasdaq weekly summary`

### Focused queries (pick based on user interest or run all)

5. `tech sector earnings results` (tech focus)
6. `venture capital funding startup` (startup ecosystem)
7. `oil price OR gold price OR commodity markets` (commodities)
8. `USD EUR currency exchange rate moves` (forex)
9. `IPO OR merger acquisition major deal` (corporate deals)
10. `recession OR economic outlook forecast` (macro outlook)

## Key Sources to Track

- European Central Bank — ecb.europa.eu/press
- Bank of Japan — boj.or.jp/en

## Evaluation Criteria

When deciding what to include in the briefing:

- **Impact**: Prioritize moves that change the macro narrative — rate changes, data surprises, major policy shifts
- **Recency**: Focus on the last 2-4 weeks unless user specifies otherwise
- **Tech relevance**: Weight toward items that affect the tech industry — funding environment, cost of capital, tech earnings, AI investment trends
- **Actionability**: Include items the user might want to act on (portfolio, career, business decisions)
- **Verification**: Use official data sources; cross-reference market moves across multiple outlets
