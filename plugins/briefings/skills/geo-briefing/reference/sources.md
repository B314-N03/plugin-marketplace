# Geopolitical Briefing Source Strategy

## Primary Search Queries

Run these searches in parallel. Always append a time qualifier based on the current date (e.g., "March 2025", "this week").

### Broad coverage (always run)

1. `world news geopolitics this week`
2. `international relations conflict diplomacy update`
3. `global economy trade sanctions`

### Regional hotspots (run all unless user specifies a focus)

4. `Middle East Iran Israel USA tensions update`
5. `Ukraine Russia war latest`
6. `China Taiwan US relations`
7. `Africa geopolitics conflict development`
8. `UAE Saudi Arabia Gulf diplomacy economy`
9. `Europe EU politics NATO`
10. `Latin America politics economy`
11. `India relations diplomacy trade`
12. `North Korea nuclear diplomacy`

### Thematic (pick based on user interest or run all)

13. `trade war tariffs sanctions new`
14. `military conflict escalation ceasefire`
15. `elections political transition`
16. `energy oil OPEC policy`
17. `global tech regulation policy`

## Source Prioritization

### Tier 1 — Primary (most reliable, check first)
- Reuters (reuters.com)
- Associated Press (apnews.com)
- BBC World (bbc.com/news/world)
- Al Jazeera (aljazeera.com)
- Foreign Affairs (foreignaffairs.com)
- The Economist (economist.com)

### Tier 2 — Analysis & depth
- Foreign Policy (foreignpolicy.com)
- CSIS (csis.org)
- Brookings (brookings.edu)
- Council on Foreign Relations (cfr.org)
- Carnegie Endowment (carnegieendowment.org)
- War on the Rocks (warontherocks.com)

### Tier 3 — Regional specialists
- Middle East Eye (middleeasteye.net)
- The Africa Report (theafricareport.com)
- South China Morning Post (scmp.com)
- Nikkei Asia (asia.nikkei.com)
- The Hindu (thehindu.com)

### German-language sources (when user writes in German)
- tagesschau.de
- Spiegel International
- NZZ (nzz.ch)
- FAZ (faz.net)

## Evaluation Criteria

- **Recency**: Focus on last 1-2 weeks unless user specifies otherwise
- **Impact**: Prioritize events that shift power dynamics, create new alliances, escalate/de-escalate conflicts, or affect global markets
- **Verification**: Cross-reference across at least 2 sources for contested claims. Flag single-source claims explicitly.
- **Neutrality**: Present multiple perspectives on contested events. Avoid editorializing on who is "right" — describe positions and actions.
- **Context**: Brief background for ongoing situations so the user can understand even if they missed previous developments
