# Space Briefing Source Strategy

## Primary Search Queries

Run these searches in parallel to cover the landscape. Always append a time qualifier like "2025" or "this week" or "March 2025" based on the current date.

### Core queries (always run)

1. `rocket launch OR space launch site:spacenews.com OR site:arstechnica.com/space OR site:nasaspaceflight.com`
2. `NASA OR ESA OR JAXA mission update discovery`
3. `James Webb Space Telescope OR JWST new discovery`
4. `SpaceX OR Starship OR Falcon launch`

### Focused queries (pick based on user interest or run all)

5. `Mars rover OR Mars mission update` (Mars focus)
6. `exoplanet discovery OR habitable zone` (astrobiology focus)
7. `commercial space company milestone` (industry focus)
8. `Artemis OR Moon mission lunar` (lunar focus)
9. `astrophysics breakthrough discovery` (science focus)
10. `space policy OR NASA budget OR space race` (policy focus)
11. `Blue Origin OR Rocket Lab OR satellite constellation` (commercial focus)

## Key Sources to Track

### Space agencies
- NASA — nasa.gov/news, blogs.nasa.gov
- ESA — esa.int/Newsroom
- JAXA — global.jaxa.jp/press
- ISRO — isro.gov.in/media
- CNSA (China) — covered via SpaceNews, NASASpaceflight

### News & analysis
- SpaceNews — spacenews.com
- Ars Technica Space — arstechnica.com/space
- NASASpaceflight — nasaspaceflight.com
- Space.com — space.com
- The Planetary Society — planetary.org/articles
- Spaceflight Now — spaceflightnow.com

### Science & research
- NASA JPL — jpl.nasa.gov/news
- ESA Science — sci.esa.int
- Nature Astronomy — nature.com/natastron (summaries from search)
- Science Magazine — science.org (summaries from search)

### Commercial space
- SpaceX — spacex.com/launches
- Blue Origin — blueorigin.com/news
- Rocket Lab — rocketlabusa.com/updates

### Launch tracking
- Next Spaceflight — nextspaceflight.com
- Launch schedule — space.com/rocket-launches

## Evaluation Criteria

When deciding what to include in the briefing:

- **Wow factor**: Prioritize genuine firsts, breakthroughs, and milestones — things that expand what humanity can do or know
- **Recency**: Focus on the last 2-4 weeks unless user specifies otherwise
- **Significance**: Skip routine operations; include new vehicles, mission milestones, scientific papers that change understanding
- **Visual appeal**: If a mission released stunning images, mention them — space is visual
- **Verification**: Use official agency sources for mission data; cross-reference discovery claims with published papers when possible
